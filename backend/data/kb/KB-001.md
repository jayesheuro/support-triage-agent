# Returns and 30-Day Window Policy

At ShopLite, our goal is to ensure you are completely satisfied with your purchase. We offer a standard **30-day return window** from the confirmed date of delivery.

## Return Eligibility Guidelines
To qualify for a standard return:
1. **Timeframe**: Return requests must be initiated within 30 calendar days of delivery.
2. **Item Condition**: Items must be unworn, unwashed, unused, and in their original packaging with all tags attached.
3. **Non-Returnable Items**: Final-sale items, personalized products, gift cards, and opened hygiene-sensitive products (e.g., earplugs, cosmetics) cannot be returned.

## Return Process
To start a return:
- Log in to your ShopLite account and navigate to **Order History**.
- Select the relevant order and click **Initiate Return**.
- Select the reason for return and download the prepaid return shipping label.

A flat return shipping fee of **$5.99** is deducted from your refund amount unless the return is due to a ShopLite error (such as a damaged, defective, or incorrect item). Once your package is scanned by the carrier, tracking updates will appear in your account.
