# Tracking Your Shipment and Delivery Statuses

Once your order has been packed and handed over to our shipping carriers, you can follow its journey directly in real time.

## Where to Find Your Tracking Number
- **Confirmation Email**: As soon as your order leaves our fulfillment center, an automated shipping confirmation email containing your direct tracking link is sent within 24 hours.
- **Account Dashboard**: Log in at ShopLite.com, visit **Account > My Orders**, and click **Track Package** next to your order.

## Understanding Carrier Statuses
- **Label Created / Pre-Shipment**: The shipping label has been printed, and your package is being sorted at our warehouse awaiting carrier pickup.
- **In Transit**: The package is moving across the carrier network and between sorting hubs.
- **Out for Delivery**: The shipment has arrived at your local post office or distribution depot and is on a courier vehicle scheduled for delivery by 8:00 PM local time.
- **Delivered**: The carrier has confirmed delivery to your doorstep, porch, mailroom, or parcel locker.

If tracking shows "Delivered" but you cannot locate the package, allow 24 hours as carriers occasionally scan parcels prematurely when entering a neighborhood.
