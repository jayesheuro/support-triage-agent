# Store Credit, Gift Card Balances, and Expiration

Understanding how your ShopLite store credit and gift cards function helps you make the most of your shopping balances.

## Balance Inquiries & Redemptions
- **Checking Balances**: You can view your active store credit or digital gift card balance at any time by logging into **My Account > Wallet & Credits**.
- **Applying Funds**: During checkout, any available store credit attached to your profile will be automatically suggested as the primary payment source.
- **Gift Card Entry**: Simply type your 16-digit gift card number and 4-digit PIN in the designated box during payment review.

## Expiration and Transfer Restrictions
- **No Expiration Date**: ShopLite store credits and gift cards **never expire** and do not incur monthly maintenance or dormancy inactivity fees.
- **Non-Transferable**: Balances cannot be transferred between different customer profiles.
- **Cash Value**: Store credits and gift cards cannot be redeemed for cash or refund checks, except where mandatory cash redemption is required by state or regional statutes.
