# Contacting a Human Support Agent and Escalations

While our automated support system resolves most inquiries instantly, you can always connect with a live human representative whenever necessary.

## Support Channels & Operating Hours
- **Live Chat**: Available directly on our website and mobile app Monday through Friday, **8:00 AM to 8:00 PM EST**, and Saturday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST.
- **Email Support**: Submit a ticket through our help portal at any time. Our human team responds within **24 hours** (or within 4 hours during standard business hours).
- **Telephone Callback**: Request a telephone callback through your account profile for complex technical or billing investigations.

## Automatic Human Escalation Triggers
Our system will automatically transfer your ticket to a dedicated human specialist under any of the following conditions:
1. Refund requests with total amounts exceeding **$20.00 USD**.
2. Claims regarding lost packages valued over $100.00.
3. Suspected account compromise, fraud, or chargeback disputes.
4. If an automated resolution has not met your needs after two attempts.
