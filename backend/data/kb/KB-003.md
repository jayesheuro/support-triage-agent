# Duplicate Charges and Billing Discrepancies

If you notice more than one charge on your bank statement for a single ShopLite order, here is how our billing system works and what steps to take.

## Pre-Authorization Holds vs. Settled Charges
- When you click "Place Order", your bank creates an initial **pre-authorization hold** to verify available funds.
- If an order attempt fails or is refreshed during checkout, your bank statement may display two pending charges. 
- Pending pre-authorizations are not actual debits; they automatically release within **24 to 48 hours** according to your bank's policy without any money leaving your account.

## Resolving Confirmed Duplicate Charges
If two identical charges have permanently posted and settled on your statement:
1. Locate your **ShopLite Order ID** and the corresponding transaction authorization numbers from your bank.
2. Contact ShopLite Support with the billing statement screenshot showing settled timestamps.
3. If the duplicate settled charge is confirmed, our billing department immediately reverses the secondary charge.
4. Duplicate reversals up to $50.00 are executed within 24 hours. Refunds above $50.00 require review by a tier-2 billing specialist.
