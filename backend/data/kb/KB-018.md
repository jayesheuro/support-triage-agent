# Subscription Orders and Auto-Delivery Management

ShopLite's Subscribe & Save program delivers recurring household and lifestyle essentials straight to your door on a flexible schedule of your choosing.

## Program Benefits & Savings
- **10% Ongoing Discount**: Save 10% on every recurring item compared to one-time purchase retail prices.
- **Flexible Cadence**: Choose delivery intervals of every 30, 60, or 90 days.
- **Free Shipping**: All subscription recurring deliveries qualify for standard free domestic shipping regardless of basket total.

## Managing Your Subscription
You retain full control over your recurring orders:
1. Navigate to **Account > Subscriptions & Auto-Delivery**.
2. You can swap product variants, change delivery frequencies, skip an upcoming delivery, or change your payment method.
3. **Cutoff Notice**: Changes or cancellations must be saved at least **48 hours prior** to your next scheduled recurring charge date to take effect for that cycle.
