# Accepted Payment Methods and Split Tender

ShopLite accepts a variety of safe, encrypted payment options during checkout to make your shopping experience seamless.

## Accepted Forms of Payment
- **Credit and Debit Cards**: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, and JCB.
- **Digital Wallets**: Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal.
- **ShopLite Gift Cards**: Digital and physical ShopLite gift cards with a valid 16-digit card number and 4-digit PIN.

## Split Tender Rules
You may combine payment methods under the following specific rules:
- You **can** split an order payment between one ShopLite Gift Card and one valid credit card or PayPal account.
- You **cannot** split a single transaction across two separate personal credit cards.
- If your gift card balance does not cover the complete order subtotal, shipping, and tax, the remaining balance will automatically be charged to your primary backup payment method.
