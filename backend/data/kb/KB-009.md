# Password Reset and Account Access Security

Protecting your ShopLite customer profile and payment details is our utmost priority. Here is how to manage and recover your account credentials.

## Resetting a Forgotten Password
1. Visit the ShopLite login page and click **Forgot your password?**.
2. Enter the email address associated with your ShopLite account.
3. Check your inbox for our automated password reset email. The secure reset link expires within **15 minutes** for security reasons.
4. Passwords must be at least 10 characters long and include an uppercase letter, a number, and a special symbol.

## Account Lockout and Security Measures
- If an account experiences **5 consecutive incorrect password attempts**, the system enforces an automatic **30-minute security lockout**.
- You cannot bypass this cooldown by contacting support until the timer expires.
- If you suspect your account has been compromised or notice unauthorized changes, immediately contact our Account Security team to freeze active sessions.
