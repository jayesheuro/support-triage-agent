# Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items Claims

If your parcel arrives broken, defective, or containing an incorrect item, ShopLite will promptly resolve the issue at no cost to you.

## How to Report an Issue
All damage or defect claims must be reported within **7 calendar days** of the confirmed carrier delivery date:
1. Navigate to **Order Support** and select your order number.
2. Choose **Report Damage or Wrong Item**.
3. Attach clear photographic evidence displaying:
   - The shipping box and shipping label showing the tracking barcode.
   - The item itself showing the defect, crack, or mismatch.

## Resolution Options
Upon receipt of your claim:
- **Immediate Replacement**: If the item is in stock, a replacement unit is expedited at zero shipping cost.
- **Full Refund**: If the item is out of stock, a full refund including original shipping is credited to your original payment method.
- **Return of Defective Item**: In most cases, we provide a prepaid return shipping label. For low-cost broken glass or non-hazardous defective goods under $20.00, our system may issue a waiver allowing you to responsibly dispose of the item without returning it.
