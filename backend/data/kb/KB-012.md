# One-Year Limited Warranty Coverage and Claims

ShopLite stands behind the durability and craftsmanship of our proprietary hardware, lifestyle, and home goods with a comprehensive warranty.

## Warranty Term and Inclusions
All ShopLite-branded products are protected by our **One-Year Limited Warranty** valid for **365 calendar days** from the verified carrier delivery date.
- **Covered**: Manufacturing defects, internal electronic hardware failure, premature component deterioration, and structural assembly defects under normal intended domestic use.
- **Not Covered**: Accidental drops, water submersion (unless the product is certified waterproof), unauthorized modifications, normal cosmetic wear and tear, or commercial usage.

## Submitting a Warranty Claim
To claim a repair, replacement, or warranty credit:
1. Contact Warranty Support with your original **ShopLite Order ID**.
2. Provide a short description and a 10-second video or high-resolution photos illustrating the defect.
3. Upon claim approval, ShopLite covers round-trip shipping for diagnostic evaluation and either repairs the item or issues a brand-new replacement within 10 business days.
