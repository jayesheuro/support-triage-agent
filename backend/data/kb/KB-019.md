# Out-of-Stock Items and Backorder Timelines

High demand occasionally causes popular ShopLite items to sell out before warehouse restocking is complete. Here is how backorders are handled.

## How Backorders Work
- If an item is listed as "Available on Backorder", you may complete checkout to reserve your position in the replenishment queue.
- Backordered merchandise is typically replenished and dispatched within **14 business days**.
- Your payment card is authorized at order submission, but settled payment capture only occurs when the backordered inventory is scanned and packaged for shipment.

## Splitting Shipments and Cancellations
- If your order contains both in-stock items and backordered goods, in-stock products ship immediately with no additional split-shipment shipping fee.
- You may cancel a backordered line item at any time before it enters fulfillment without penalty by visiting **Order Details** and clicking **Cancel Item**.
