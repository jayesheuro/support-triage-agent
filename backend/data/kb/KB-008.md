# Lost or Stolen Packages and Carrier Claims

Experiencing a missing delivery can be frustrating. Follow these verification steps to file a claim for packages marked delivered that cannot be found.

## 24-Hour Delivery Verification Period
Carriers sometimes mark a delivery as complete while approaching your delivery area or leaving the package in an unexpected location. Before opening an investigation:
1. Verify the delivery address on your order confirmation email.
2. Check alternative delivery spots: porch, side doors, garage, leasing office, and behind hedges.
3. Inquire with neighbors or household members.
4. Allow **24 hours** from the timestamp of delivery before opening a formal carrier loss ticket.

## Carrier Investigation & Claim Filing
If 24 hours have elapsed and the parcel remains unlocated:
- Submit a **Missing Delivery Claim** through customer support.
- Orders valued at **$100.00 or less** are eligible for an immediate complimentary one-time replacement or store credit.
- Orders valued **over $100.00** require an official carrier loss trace and signature verification by a human support specialist before a re-shipment or refund can be authorized.
