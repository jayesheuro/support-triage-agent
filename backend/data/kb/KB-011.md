# Promotional Codes, Coupons, and Stacking Rules

We frequently run seasonal promotions, newsletter discounts, and sitewide deals to reward our valued shoppers.

## Applying a Promotional Code
1. Add qualifying items to your shopping cart.
2. At checkout, expand the **Promo Code / Gift Card** field located under your order summary.
3. Enter the exact code (case-sensitive) and click **Apply**.
4. The discount amount will be reflected in your order total before finalizing payment.

## Promotional Rules & Limitations
- **Stacking Restrictions**: Only **one promotional discount code** may be applied per order. Promo codes cannot be stacked together with other promotional codes or flash-sale coupons.
- **Exclusions**: Promo codes cannot be redeemed on gift card purchases, clearance items, or select electronics unless explicitly stated.
- **Retroactive Applications**: If you forgot to enter a valid promo code on an order placed within the last **24 hours**, contact customer support with your Order ID and code; our team will verify eligibility and apply a post-purchase store credit.
