# International Shipping, Duties, and Customs

ShopLite currently provides reliable global shipping to select international destinations, including Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union, Australia, and New Zealand.

## International Delivery Timelines
- **Standard International Shipping**: Estimated delivery time is **7 to 14 business days**, depending on customs processing in the destination country.
- **Express International Shipping**: Estimated delivery time is **3 to 5 business days** via DHL Express.

## Customs, Import Duties, and Local Taxes
- All prices displayed on ShopLite.com are listed in USD and do not include foreign value-added taxes (VAT), import tariffs, or brokerage surcharges unless specified as DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) at checkout.
- Customers are responsible for any customs assessments levied by the border authority in their destination jurisdiction.
- If an international shipment is rejected by the recipient due to unexpected customs fees, return shipping expenses and a 15% restocking fee will be deducted from any final refund.
