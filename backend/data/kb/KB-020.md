# Security, Two-Factor Authentication, and Suspicious Activity

ShopLite employs rigorous multi-layered defense to safeguard customer accounts, identity data, and stored payment tokens.

## Enabling Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
We strongly recommend activating 2FA for all customer accounts:
1. Navigate to **Account Settings > Security & Privacy**.
2. Toggle on **Two-Factor Authentication**.
3. Scan the QR code using any standard authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, or 1Password) and enter the 6-digit confirmation code.
4. Save your emergency backup recovery codes in a secure location offline.

## Reporting Suspicious Activity
If you observe unauthorized profile modifications, unexplained order confirmations, or password change emails:
- Contact our dedicated **Security Incident Desk** immediately.
- Our security protocols trigger an instant freeze on account activity, revoke all active mobile/web sessions, and temporarily block outbound shipments on pending orders associated with your profile.
- A human fraud specialist will verify identity credentials with you before restoring account privileges.
