# Shipping Methods, Timelines, and Delay Protocols

ShopLite partners with major shipping carriers to deliver your orders promptly and reliably across the continental United States.

## Standard and Expedited Shipping Tiers
- **Standard Shipping (3–5 business days)**: Free for orders of $45.00 or more; otherwise a flat $4.99 fee applies. Orders are dispatched from our distribution center within 24 hours of purchase on weekdays.
- **Express Shipping (1–2 business days)**: Flat fee of $14.99. Orders placed before **2:00 PM EST** Monday through Friday ship the same business day.

## Shipping Cutoffs & Holidays
Our fulfillment centers operate Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST. Orders placed after 2:00 PM EST on Friday, or during weekends and federal holidays, will be processed on the following business day.

## Delay Protocols
In rare cases of severe weather disruptions, carrier backlog, or peak holiday volumes, shipments may experience minor delays.
- If your shipment has not updated within **7 business days** of dispatch, contact support.
- If a package remains un-scanned by the carrier for more than **10 business days**, ShopLite classifies the order as lost in transit and will immediately dispatch a free replacement or initiate a refund.
