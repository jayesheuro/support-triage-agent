# Price Match Guarantee and Adjustment Windows

We want our customers to shop with confidence knowing they are getting the best value on ShopLite products.

## ShopLite Post-Purchase Price Adjustments
If an item you purchased drops in price on ShopLite.com within **7 calendar days** of your purchase date, we will gladly credit you the difference:
1. Contact customer support with your **Order ID** and the link to the reduced product page.
2. The item must be identical in model, color, size, and condition.
3. The difference will be credited as ShopLite store credit or refunded to your original payment method within 3 business days.

## Competitor Price Matching Exceptions
ShopLite does not match prices from unauthorized third-party marketplace sellers (e.g., unauthorized sellers on eBay or Amazon Marketplace), clearance blowouts, liquidations, or limited-quantity "doorbuster" promotional events.
