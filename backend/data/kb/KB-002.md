# Refund Rules and Auto-Approval Thresholds

ShopLite processes refunds according to strict compliance and dollar-value authorization policies to ensure customer satisfaction while preventing fraud.

## Refund Approval Tiers
1. **Low-Value Refunds (≤ $20.00)**:
   - Eligible for immediate **auto-approval** by automated support systems or front-line assistance.
   - Applies to damaged minor items, missing accessories, or approved order cancellations where total compensation is less than or equal to $20.00 USD.
   - No manager or human agent review is required for claims up to this limit.
2. **High-Value Refunds (> $20.00)**:
   - Any refund request exceeding **$20.00 USD** strictly requires **human agent review and manual authorization**.
   - Automated agents cannot grant or promise full refunds exceeding $20.00 without routing the ticket to the Human Approval Queue.

## Processing Timelines & Method
- All refunds are remitted directly to the **original payment method** used at checkout.
- Credit and debit card refunds typically settle within **3 to 5 business days**, depending on your financial institution.
- Store credit refunds are issued immediately upon approval.
- Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return was necessitated by an internal warehouse error or carrier damage.
