# Changing Shipping Address Post-Order

Entering an incorrect shipping destination can lead to misdelivered packages. Because orders enter automated processing quickly, address edits are time-sensitive.

## Address Modification Window
You can update your destination address directly within **30 minutes** of submitting your order:
1. Go to **My Orders** and locate the recent order.
2. If the status is still **Pending**, click **Update Shipping Address**.
3. Enter and save the verified new domestic delivery address.

## After the 30-Minute Grace Window
Once an order updates to **Processing** or has reached warehouse packing:
- Neither automated tools nor customer support agents can alter the destination address in our warehouse fulfillment queue.
- If the carrier provides package interception (such as UPS My Choice or FedEx Delivery Manager), you may sign in to the carrier's platform with your tracking number and arrange a delivery redirection or hold for pickup at a local facility.
