# Order Cancellation Windows and Rules

Because ShopLite relies on an automated warehouse fulfillment system designed to pack and dispatch merchandise quickly, cancellation windows are strictly time-bound.

## The 30-Minute Cancellation Window
You may cancel an order directly from your account within **30 minutes** of placing it:
1. Go to **My Orders** within 30 minutes of purchase.
2. If your order is still in **Pending** status, click the **Cancel Order** button.
3. Your order will be immediately cancelled, and any pending credit card authorization hold will be released within 24 to 48 hours.

## In-Processing and Shipped Orders
Once an order passes the 30-minute grace period or updates to **Processing**, **Fulfilling**, or **Shipped**, automated cancellations can no longer be executed by automated systems or human support agents. 
- At this stage, warehouse workers have already picked, packed, or palletized the order.
- To recover funds after this point, please wait for delivery and initiate a standard return within the 30-day window using our self-service returns portal.
